How this works

The dashboard can keep showing your current overall form, while this page stores each season by year. When 2027 rounds are added, 2026 remains archived here. Older 2025-and-back GHIN scores can be added as score-only rounds, so they count toward score history and records without affecting GIR, fairway, putting, or hole-by-hole averages.
